There are 3 Hocruxes that need to be destroy which are the cup, the diadem, and the diary. You can destroy
the cup and the diadem straight away. For the diary, you will need to get the basilisk venom which can be
obtained by killing the Basilisk in the Chamber of Secrets.
The path leading to some more hidden areas are "secretpath" from Slytherin to Chamber of Secrets, "outside"
from Chamber of Secrets to the Last Hocrux, "the top of the castle" to Final Boss.
You need to defeat Voldemort and also destroy all the Hocruxes to win.
